Your privacy matters to us.

Your health logs stay securely on your phone unless you choose to share them. Chats are encrypted to keep conversations private between participants. Your contact details are visible only to you and the house admins. Your information is always yours 💙.

More details
YOUR DATA AND PRIVACY
On your phone›

Health logs, calorie entries, readings and medicines.

Checklists, tasks and notes, and your preferences (feed choices, countdown, ship tools).

A saved copy of the house screens so Rules, Wi-Fi and contacts still open offline, and your sign-in on this phone.

If you tap Allow for weather, your location is rounded to about 100 m and kept on this phone. It is never sent to the house server. It is sent to outside services only to get your forecast (see Outside services below).

Health data never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

On the house server›

Your profile, house status and standing, and your payment records.

Receipts and payment proofs, in a private storage area that is not public.

Your posts, Crowd posts and comments, and bug reports or ideas you send.

Messages to the HouseMaster (see Messages below).

Your member chats, encrypted (see Messages below).

When you were last active in the app. Housemates see “Active now” or “Active 3d ago” on your profile; turn off “Show when I am online” in Me and nothing is recorded or shown.

The devices you sign in on: the type of phone and browser, and when each was last used, so you and the main admins can spot misuse. No location or IP address is kept.

House manual tips you send (an admin approves them before anyone else sees them).

Who can see what›

Housemates: names, photos and house status. Never another member’s phone, email, emergency contact, birthdate or age (members the admins give finance access to can see contact details, like admins). A housemate’s birthday shows as month and day only.

Everyone signed in: the HouseMaster on duty’s phone, and the shared emergency, utility and supplier numbers.

House admins: member contacts, the records needed to run the house (dues, payments, receipts, rent fund), and messages sent to the HouseMaster.

Main admin only: usage counts (which screens are opened, and how often) and bug reports.

Nobody in the app can read member-to-member chats except the people in that chat.

Messages›

Chats between members. Stored encrypted on the server. They are read only through functions that check you are in that chat, so the house admins cannot read them in the app. They are not in the Drive backup.

Messages to the HouseMaster. These go to the HouseMaster and the house admins and are stored as normal text. The first 160 characters show in the HouseMaster’s inbox and push notice. They are included in the nightly Drive backup once it is switched on.

Backups›

Once the main admin switches it on, a nightly private backup of the house records goes to the house Google Drive (1:30 AM Manila time). It is off until switched on.

Included: members, imported batches, payments, rent fund, house settings, agreements, audit log, profile updates, repair tickets, Crowd posts and comments, posts, messages to the HouseMaster, member aliases, activities and bug reports, plus payment receipt images.

Not included: member-to-member chats. The backup sits in a confidential folder and is not shared publicly.

Bug report screenshots›

A screenshot you attach is stored in private storage under your own folder. Only you and the house admins can open it.

The bug report text is seen by you and the main admin.

Outside services›

Weather: if you choose to use your location, a rounded location (about 100 m) goes to open-meteo.com for the forecast and to api.bigdatacloud.net to look up the place name. Nothing else about you. Without it, the forecast is for the house.

Weather map: when you tap to show it, a rounded location (or the house) goes to windy.com to draw the map.

Food search: the words or barcode you search for go to world.openfoodfacts.org. If the house switches on FatSecret search, your search words also go through the house server to platform.fatsecret.com (credited on screen); your phone does not contact FatSecret directly.

Exercise pictures in the workout log load from raw.githubusercontent.com.

Maps and Messenger: the house map is from Google Maps. Messenger and other links open the other service when you tap them.

Every website you load also sees your phone’s internet address. The app’s own code libraries load from unpkg.com. Other services the app calls without sending anything about you: open.er-api.com (exchange rates), marine-api.open-meteo.com (tides for the house), icanhazdadjoke.com (joke of the day) and cdn.jsdelivr.net (only when you run the speed test). If the main admin turns on the Drive filer, files you upload there go to the house’s own Google Drive script together with your sign-in.

Never collected›

No location tracking: location is used only for weather, only if you allow it, and is not stored on the server.

Your phone contacts are never read.

Your browsing or other apps are never tracked.

Correction or deletion›

To correct or delete your information, message the main admin or the app developer. We will fix it or remove it.
