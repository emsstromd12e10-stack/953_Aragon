Hi, Chief Financial Admin

Your money tools, step by step.

1. Admin tab2. Receipts3. Record payment4. Annual due5. Reminders6. Transactions7. Records review8. Status list9. Devices10. Exports11. Main admin

You are the Chief Financial Admin, so you confirm what comes in and you decide the membership labels. Your tabs are Feed, Members, Admin, Me, with the gold + in the middle. Tap Admin for everything below.

1Your Admin tab and the Action board

The Action board is the first tile. It brings together everything that waits for you.

Where things are

  1. Action board: what to decide today.
  2. Renewals: dues, receipts to confirm, reminders.
  3. Transactions: every record.
  4. Records review: imported payment history.
  5. Status of memberships: the status list.
  1. Action board.
  2. Renewals.

Know your House app

  1. Admin tab, then the Know your House app tile: it is the last tile in the HOUSE section, below Rules & agreement.
  2. It opens the picture guide for your role inside the app. Tap Back to return.
  1. Know your House app.

Decide from the Action board

  1. The top line says how many things wait for a decision. Rows are oldest first.
  2. Suggested new members, Under review and Earlier years unpaid: Approve or Decline on the row itself (tap twice to confirm).
  3. Payments to confirm and Records review open their own screens.
  4. Members in arrears, Members missing details, Repair reports and Devices are listed for information. The tile’s number counts decisions only.
  1. How many to decide.
  2. Approve.
  3. Decline.

The house contract reminder

  1. No house contract uploaded yet? A card on the Feed says House contract not uploaded yet.
  2. You see it with the HouseMaster on duty, only on the Feed’s All tab.
  3. Tap Upload now to go to the House contract card. Tap Later to hide it for today.
  4. It disappears once a contract is uploaded.

What’s new for you

  1. On the Feed, the NEW IN THE APP card shows only what applies to you.
  2. As an admin you also see an item about the Know your House app tile.
  3. Tap Got it at the top to hide it.

2Confirm or reject receipts

When a member posts a dues receipt it waits for you.

Open Renewals

  1. Admin tab, then Renewals.
  2. The top shows how many have renewed.
  3. Remind all unpaid sends a reminder to every unpaid member.
  4. Record payment + receipt adds a payment yourself.
  1. Remind all unpaid.
  2. Record payment + receipt.

Confirm or reject

  1. Scroll to RECEIPTS TO CONFIRM.
  2. Check the amount and reference against the receipt photo.
  3. Confirm if right. Reject if wrong.
  4. If nothing changed (someone already confirmed or rejected it), the app tells you instead of saying done.
  1. Reject.
  2. Confirm.

Confirming is what turns a member Active, so check carefully.

3Record a payment

For cash, or a receipt that came in Messenger.

Add the record

  1. Renewals, then Record payment + receipt. (Or + Record payment in Transactions.)
  2. Pick the member, the type (Dues, Utilities, Repair, Purchase), amount, method and reference.
  3. Set Confirmed, Pending or Rejected.
  4. Attach the receipt photo if you have one.
  5. Tap Save record.
  1. Save record.

4Set the annual due

One amount and one deadline for everyone.

Change amount or deadline

  1. Renewals, then scroll to the annual due.
  2. Type the amount and choose the deadline.
  3. Tap Save annual due.
  1. Save annual due.

5Renewals and reminders

The app can remind members before the deadline.

Automatic reminders

  1. In Renewals, find Automatic reminders.
  2. Pick how many days before the deadline: 60, 30, 14, 7, 1.
  3. Optional: Also nudge sponsors of unpaid members.
  4. Below, each member row has Remind, Receipt and Mark paid.
  1. The reminder days.
  2. Nudge sponsors.

6Transactions

Every record in one list.

Find, edit, export

  1. Admin tab, then Transactions.
  2. Filter: All, Dues, Utilities, Repairs, Pending.
  3. Tap a record to open it, change it, or set Confirmed or Rejected, then Save record.
  4. Export CSV downloads the list.
  1. Pending: waiting for you.
  2. Record payment.
  3. Export CSV.

7Records review and the import batch

Payment history rebuilt from the Messenger receipts group and the old sheet.

Check each record

  1. Admin tab, then Records review.
  2. FLAGGED rows need a look first.
  3. Tick rows and use Confirm, Reject, Note or Edit. Every action can be undone and is in the audit log.
  1. Flagged records.
  2. Select all.

The one-time import batch (import-2026-10)

  1. At the bottom of Records review, a card says Confirm all unflagged imported records. It confirms every imported record without a flag in one go. Flagged ones stay pending.
  2. You can only do this once. The app asks you to confirm.
  3. Important: membership statuses read correctly only after you confirm this batch. Until then, older payments are not counted.

8Membership status: suggestions, approval and hand-set

You, the main admin and the admins can read the list. Only you and the main admin decide the labels. The app suggests; you say yes.

Read the list

  1. Admin tab, then Status of memberships.
  2. Counts at the top. Filters: All, Active, In arrears, Under review, Review needed, Proposed, and Was ‘New member’ before.
  3. Nobody is “New member” by themselves any more. The app only suggests it for members added from now on, and you approve or tag by hand.
  4. Member in Arrears stays automatic. You get one notice per member per year when someone becomes in arrears.
  1. Proposed: suggestions waiting for you.
  2. Was ‘New member’ before.
  3. Review needed.

Approve or decline a suggestion

  1. Open the Proposed filter (or use the Action board).
  2. Suggested New member, Under review (three or more unpaid years in a row) and the admin-only tag for earlier years unpaid each wait for your Approve or Decline.
  3. Until you approve, the member shows Active or Member in Arrears, with no label. A declined suggestion is not repeated for the same reason.
  4. The member is never told about a suggestion. A member who has already paid this year is not suggested as “New member”.
  1. Approve the proposal.
  2. Decline the proposal.

Set a status by hand

  1. Tap Set status by hand next to the member.
  2. Type a note (at least 3 characters), then tap Active, In arrears or Under review.
  3. The note is visible to all admins, not to the member. The member sees only the label.
  4. The row says Set by hand. Tap Back to automatic to return it to the automatic status.
  1. The note.
  2. Choose the status.

Members who used to show “New member”

  1. Before this release, some members showed “Under review - New member”. If they have not paid this year they now show Member in Arrears, like every other unpaid member.
  2. Open the filter Was ‘New member’ before and tag the right ones with Tag as new member. The tag ends by itself when something is confirmed paid for the year.
  1. The filter.

Open and close a review

  1. Tap Open review next to the member.
  2. Write a reason (at least 3 characters). Tap Save review. They move to Under review.
  3. When it is settled, tap Close the review.
  1. The reason.
  2. Save review.

9Devices report

Spot a PIN shared too widely, or a lost phone. For you and the main admin.

Read the report

  1. Admin tab, then Devices.
  2. Accounts with unusual devices come first: more than 3 active devices, or 3 or more new devices in 24 hours.
  3. Each device shows the type of phone and browser and when it was last used. No location or IP address is kept.
  4. A flag only alerts. Nothing is blocked automatically.

Block and unblock

  1. Tap Block next to a device, then confirm. That phone is signed out the next time it opens and cannot sign in again.
  2. Tap Unblock to let it back in.
  3. Lost or stolen phone? Also reset the member’s PIN.

PIN lockout alerts

  1. Five wrong PINs lock that account for 15 minutes.
  2. The member, the main admin and the Chief Financial Admin each get an Inbox notice. Tap yours to open the Devices report.
  3. If the member says it was not them, check their devices and reset the PIN.

  1. Export 2026–27 payment records (CSV).
