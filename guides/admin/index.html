Admin guide

The tools every admin has.

1. Admin tab2. HouseMaster3. Repairs4. Announcements5. Calendar6. Manuals7. Birthdays8. Numbers9. Renewals10. Status list11. Lotto12. Contract

For regular admins. Your bottom tabs are Feed, Members, Admin, Me, with the gold + in the middle. You can see who has renewed and send reminders, and you can record utilities, repairs and purchases. Confirming receipts, dues totals, the Ledger · money flow tile, records review and devices belong to the Chief Financial Admin and the main admin.

1Your Admin tab and the Action board

Tap Admin at the bottom. A bar says Admin mode, with Switch to member view if you want to see what members see. The Action board is the first tile.

The tools in this guide

  1. The Action board tile shows how many things wait for a decision.
  2. Check the numbers, Post an announcement, Birthdays, Status of memberships, Missing details and Lotto updates are all here.
  3. Scroll down for the HOUSE section: HouseMaster, House manuals, Calendar, Repairs, Rules & agreement.
  4. On the Feed, the NEW IN THE APP card shows only what applies to you. As an admin you also see an item about the Know your House app tile.
  5. A tile that is still loading says Checking... and keeps its place. If it says Not available yet, tap it to try again.
  1. Action board.
  2. Check the numbers.

Know your House app

  1. Admin tab, then the Know your House app tile: it is the last tile in the HOUSE section, below Rules & agreement.
  2. It opens the picture guide for your role inside the app. Tap Back to return.
  1. Know your House app.

The Action board

  1. It lists what is waiting, oldest first, with how old the oldest item is. Tap a row to deal with it.
  2. A regular admin sees only what an admin may handle: profile updates, repairs, house manual tips and similar. The money rows and the status suggestions are for the Chief Financial Admin and the main admin.
  3. If you are the HouseMaster on duty you also see Status requests: a retired or inactive member who marked “At 953 C. Aragon” waits for a yes. The HouseMaster on duty, a finance officer or the main admin can approve or decline it, and the member is told.
  4. The number on the tile counts things to decide. Information rows (repairs, devices, members in arrears, members missing details) are listed but not counted.
  1. How many to decide.

2HouseMaster duty and messages

The HouseMaster is the first admin in the house order who is AT 953 C. Aragon.

Who is on duty

  1. Admin tab, then HouseMaster.
  2. It names the admin on duty.
  3. On duty yourself? Members’ messages arrive here and in your Inbox, and the HouseMaster can call and mark them done.
  4. Set your status to AT 953 C. Aragon (the chip under your name) to be in the order. Away or onboard hands duty to the next admin.
  1. The admin on duty.
  2. Message the HouseMaster.

3Repairs

Members report repairs. You take them from there.

Assign, resolve, reopen

  1. Admin tab, then Repairs.
  2. Assign to me takes it. Add a cost if there is one.
  3. Resolve closes it. Reopen brings it back.
  1. Assign to me.
  2. Resolve.
  3. Reopen.

4Announcements and urgent alerts

Tap the gold +, then Announcement or Urgent alert.

Post an announcement

  1. Gold +, then Announcement.
  2. Pick a type, a title and a message.
  3. Choose Everyone or AT 953 C. ARAGON only.
  4. Optional: Pin to top of feed, or a push notification.
  5. Tap Publish.
  1. The type: Announcement, Reminder, Utility reminder and more.
  2. Who sees it.
  3. Publish.

Send an urgent alert

  1. Gold +, then Urgent alert. It goes out as a push to every phone.
  2. Keep the title short and the message clear.
  3. Tap Publish.
  1. Urgent alert.
  2. Publish.

5Calendar notices

Power and water interruptions show on the Calendar.

Check and add notices

  1. Admin tab, then Calendar.
  2. Scroll to POWER & WATER INTERRUPTIONS. Notices checked automatically from Meralco and Maynilad appear here.
  3. A notice that is over is hidden for everyone. Tap Show ended notices to see them again.
  4. Wrong or finished? Tap Remove. Missed one? Tap Add a notice from an official source.
  1. Show ended notices.
  2. Add a notice.

6House manuals approval

Members send tips for a house manual. Nothing goes live until an admin approves it.

Approve or reject

  1. Admin tab, then House manuals.
  2. New tips wait under TO REVIEW.
  3. Read it. Approve, Edit, or Reject (say why).
  1. The tip waiting for review.
  2. Reject.
  3. Approve.

7Birthdays and missing details

Two small kindnesses.

Birthdays

  1. Admin tab, then Birthdays.
  2. See today’s birthdays and the next 60 days.
  3. Tap a name to schedule a greeting.
  1. Today’s birthday.
  2. A coming birthday. Tap to greet.

The automatic greeting

  1. Further down, the Automatic birthday greeting sends a message on the day.
  2. Write NAME where the first name should go. [Name] and [Member’s Name] work too.
  3. The preview shows the message with an example name.
  4. Choose Just them or Whole house, then Save automatic greeting.
  1. The message. Use NAME for the first name.
  2. The preview.

Missing details reminders

  1. Admin tab, then Missing details.
  2. The filters show how many members miss each item (All, Birthday, Mobile number and so on). You see which items are missing, never the details themselves.
  3. Select all or tick members, then Remind selected. Copy names copies the names of the members shown, to paste into a chat.
  1. Totals: tap one to filter.
  2. Copy names.
  3. Remind selected.

8Check the numbers

Automatic checks that the totals in the app add up.

Read the result

  1. Admin tab, then Check the numbers.
  2. Green means every check passes.
  3. A red line means a total does not add up. Tell the Chief Financial Admin.
  4. Money totals such as Dues collected show only for the main admin and the finance admin, so you will see fewer lines than they do.
  1. The overall result.

9Renewals, and Transactions for utilities, repairs and purchases

Renewals and Transactions touch money, with limits. Dues payments stay with the Chief Financial Admin and the main admin.

Renewals: who has renewed, and reminders

  1. Admin tab, then Renewals. The tile says how many have renewed for the year, for example 12/30.
  2. The top card shows the count renewed. The peso totals are for the main admin only.
  3. WHO HAS RENEWED lists every member who is not retired. A paid member has a ✓ PAID tag. For an unpaid member, tap Remind to send that one member a reminder.
  4. Remind all unpaid sends the reminder to every unpaid member, to their phone and Inbox. Status of memberships opens the status list.
  5. You will not see Mark paid, Receipt, Record payment + receipt or RECEIPTS TO CONFIRM. Save annual due and the CSV export say “Only finance can ...”. Ask the Chief Financial Admin.

Transactions: utilities, repairs and purchases

  1. Admin tab, then Transactions. It shows the records for Utilities, Repairs and Purchases. Dues records are not shown to you.
  2. The totals at the top are PURCHASES, UTILITIES and REPAIRS SPENT.
  3. Tap a row to edit it. Tap + Record payment to add a new one. First choose the type: Utilities, Repair or Purchase (Dues is not in the list). Then check the member and the amount, and tap Save record.
  4. Only the main admin can delete a record. If one should be removed, tell the main admin.

10Membership status list

Admins can see everyone’s status: Active, In arrears or Under review.

Read the list

  1. Admin tab, then Status of memberships.
  2. Counts at the top. Filter by All, Active, In arrears or Under review.
  3. “Under review” and the admin-only tag for earlier years unpaid show only after the Chief Financial Admin or the main admin has approved them. “Member in Arrears” is automatic.
  4. Open review adds a reason (at least 3 characters). Close the review when it is settled.
  5. Statuses come from dues the finance admin has confirmed. If one looks wrong, ask the finance admin.
  1. Filter by status.
  2. Open a review for a member.

11Lotto updates

Members read the results in Tools. They update by themselves; you only look after the switch.

The automatic update

  1. Admin tab, then Lotto updates.
  2. The Automatic fetch switch turns the automatic update on or off. You cannot type or change a result here; results come only from the automatic update.
  3. It reads an unofficial results page, so members can check the official PCSO site if a result looks wrong. It switches itself off after 12 failed runs in a row; turn it on again here.
  4. FETCH LOG, below the switch, shows each run.
  1. The automatic update switch.

12House contract and stories

Two things members see that admins look after.

Upload a new house contract

  1. Admin tab, then Rules & agreement. Find the House contract card.
  2. Tap Upload new house contract (PDF), choose the file and add a note.
  3. Replacing it warns you first. Every member then sees the new contract under House.
  1. The House contract card.

The house contract reminder

  1. No contract uploaded yet? A card on the Feed says House contract not uploaded yet.
  2. Only the HouseMaster on duty and the finance admin see it, and only on the Feed’s All tab.
  3. Tap Upload now to go to the House contract card. Tap Later to hide it for today.
  4. It disappears once a contract is uploaded.

Stories: what to know

  1. A story lasts 24 hours. Each member can post 10 a day.
  2. The author chooses who sees it: all housemates, chosen members, or only themselves.
  3. A photo on a story for chosen members or only the author is hidden in the app. The file is stored under a random name in public storage, so anyone who is sent the exact link can open it.
